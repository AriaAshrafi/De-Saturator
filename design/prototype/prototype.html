Drag a knob up or down; Shift drags finely, double-click resets, the wheel steps it once you've clicked in the window. On the analyser: double-click to add a point (up to six), drag it, scroll on it to change its width, double-click it to remove it. With a point selected, the arrow keys move it and Delete removes it. D toggles Delta, B Bypass. Rest the pointer on anything for its tooltip.
