De-Saturator

The window as designed, wired to the spec. The analyser runs on a stand-in signal and the thinning is a sketch of the look; nothing plays.

States
Size
Signal

Drag a knob up or down; Shift drags finely, double-click resets, the wheel steps it once you've clicked in the window. On the analyser: double-click to add a point (up to six), drag it, scroll on it to change its width, double-click it to remove it. Rest the pointer on anything for its tooltip. Light and Dark, bottom right, switch the window's theme. The plugin takes no keys, so the host keeps its shortcuts.
